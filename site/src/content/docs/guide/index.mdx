pagecue gives you speaker notes for any web page: the report, dashboard or site you made with Claude and now have to present. Your notes, called **cues**, sit in a small window of their own, the **prompter**. As you scroll the page, the prompter shows the cue for the part on screen.

You share only the page. The prompter stays on your side.

<Shot eager name="prompter" alt="The pagecue prompter: the current cue, September doubled March, is lit, with the cues before and after it dimmed." width={320} />

## How the pieces fit

pagecue has two parts. You need the first; the second lets Claude write your cues.

- **The Chrome extension** opens the prompter for the tab you're on and keeps it in step with the page, in both directions: scroll the page and the prompter follows; click a cue and the page scrolls to it.
- **The Claude plugin** lets Claude write the cues. Tell Claude what you want to say; it reads the page, plans where each cue goes, and writes a headline and up to three points for each. While you rehearse, it can change them on the spot.

You can also write and edit every cue yourself, right in the prompter.

## The short version

1. Ask Claude: "Add presenter cues to this page."
2. Open the page and click pagecue.
3. In your meeting, share the page's tab, not your screen.

## What a cue is

A cue covers one horizontal part of the page, from where it starts down to where the next cue starts. Each has:

- a **headline** of about eight words, the point you're making;
- up to **three short points**: a number to say, why it matters, an answer to a likely question.

One cue is current at a time: the one whose part has reached the **reading line**, about a third of the way down the window.
