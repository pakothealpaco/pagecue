title: Share your screen privately
description: Share only the page in Zoom, Google Meet or Microsoft Teams, so viewers see the page and you see your cues.

The prompter is a separate window. When you share **only the page's tab or window**, viewers see the page and nothing else. The prompter is never part of what you share.

<Aside type="caution" title="Don't share your whole screen">
If you share your entire screen, the prompter is on it, like any other window.
</Aside>

## In your meeting app

<Tabs syncKey="meeting">
<TabItem label="Google Meet">

Start presenting, and in Chrome's picker choose the **Chrome tab** with your page. A shared tab is the cleanest choice: viewers see only that tab, even if other windows cover it.

</TabItem>
<TabItem label="Zoom">

Click **Share Screen** and pick the Chrome window that holds your page, not the whole screen. Keep the page in a Chrome window of its own, apart from the prompter.

</TabItem>
<TabItem label="Microsoft Teams">

Click **Share** and pick the Chrome window that holds your page, not the whole screen.

</TabItem>
</Tabs>

Menu names change from version to version; what matters is choosing a tab or a window, never a screen.

## Set up your screens

- **One screen:** put the page on the left and the prompter, narrow, on the right, close to your camera so your eyes don't wander far.
- **Two screens:** share the page on one, keep the prompter on the other, at eye level.

## What viewers see

| On the page | Viewers see it? |
|---|---|
| The page itself | Yes |
| The prompter window | No, it isn't shared |
| Section lines (<kbd>L</kbd>) | **Yes**: they're drawn on the page. Turn them off before you present. |
| Scrolling when you click a cue or press a key | Yes, the page scrolls as if you scrolled it |

pagecue draws nothing on the page except section lines, and only while you turn them on.

## Before you start

1. Open the page and the prompter. Check the cue light is green.
2. Press <kbd>L</kbd> if the section lines are on, so they're off.
3. Share the tab or window, not the screen.
4. Click the timer to start it from zero.
