Every cue is yours to change, right in the prompter, whoever wrote it.

## Change a cue's words

Double-click a cue, or press <kbd>Enter</kbd> on the current one.

<Shot eager name="editing" markers="editing" alt="A cue open for editing, with the headline, points, Delete and Done." width={420} />

| # | Part | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Headline** | The cue's main line. <kbd>Enter</kbd> moves to the points. |
| 2 | **Points** | One point per line. A leading `-`, `*` or `•` is fine; it's tidied away. |
| 3 | **Delete** | Removes the cue. **Undo** in the note that follows brings it back. |
| 4 | **Done** | Saves (<kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>Enter</kbd>, or <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> <kbd>Enter</kbd>). |

Clicking anywhere else also saves. <kbd>Esc</kbd> cancels and puts the words back. There's no Undo for your own wording once saved.

Your edits win from then on: if Claude or the page's own cue file later changes that cue, your wording stays.

<Aside type="note">
Cues in Hebrew, Arabic and other right-to-left languages read right to left automatically, cue by cue.
</Aside>

## Add a cue

Scroll the page to where the new part should start, so it's at the reading line, then do any of these:

- press <kbd>N</kbd>;
- double-click empty space in the prompter;
- choose **Add a cue here** in the ··· menu;
- with [section lines](/guide/section-lines/) on, click **+ Add cue here** on the page.

The cue opens for you to write. Leave it empty and it goes away.

If there is nothing at the reading line to attach to, the prompter says so: scroll a little and try again.

## Delete a cue

Open it for editing and click **Delete**. A note says **Cue deleted.** with **Undo**, for five seconds.

## Move cues to another computer

Open the ··· menu:

- **Export cues…** saves this page's cues as `<page-title>.cues.json`, including any the page can't place right now.
- **Add cues from Claude…** adds cues Claude gave you, pasted or from a cue file, to the current page. Cues with the same id are replaced, and imported cues count as your own edits.

The file format is described in [Cue file format](/guide/reference/cue-file/).

## When a cue can't find its place

Pages change. A cue whose part of the page is gone isn't moved somewhere else; it's left out, and a note under the list says how many: "2 cues aren't on this page." Ask Claude to update the cues.

If none of a page's cues can be placed, the prompter lists them all anyway, without following the page. Move through them with <kbd>↑</kbd> and <kbd>↓</kbd> while you present, and ask Claude to update them afterwards.
