You don't need special words; Claude understands what you mean. These are starting points.

## Writing cues

> Add presenter cues to this report. I'm presenting it to our retail partners on Thursday.

> Add speaker notes to report.html. On the revenue chart: September doubled March because of the cold brew launch. On the risks: both are handled, don't dwell.

> Add cues to `https://example.com/pricing`. I want to stress that annual plans save two months.

> Write cues for this dashboard. You write them; keep each to one number and why it matters.

> הוסף שליפים לדוח הזה, בעברית.

Give Claude what only you know: who you're talking to, the one thing they should remember, the questions you expect. It takes the facts from the page.

## Changing cues

> Make cue 3 shorter.

> Add a cue here: we match any quote for the first year.

> Add a cue at "Where it sold" thanking Dana's team.

> Move the question about margins to the pricing cue.

> Rewrite all the cues for a five-minute version.

> Turn off the section lines and make the text bigger.

## Good cues

- **One point per headline.** "September doubled March", not "Revenue".
- **Numbers you'll say out loud.** Exact, and the ones that matter.
- **Up to three points.** You'll glance at them for half a second.
- **The likely question.** "Q: Seasonal? → Oct tracking +6%."

Claude follows these by default. If a cue is still too long, say so.
