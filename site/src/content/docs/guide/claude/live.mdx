Rehearsing is when you find out a cue is too long, or a point is missing. With the prompter open, just tell Claude. It knows which page you're on, sees every cue with your own edits, and knows which one is current.

> Add a cue here: we match any quote for the first year.
>
> Make cue 3 shorter, and turn off the section lines.

"Here" and "this part" mean the cue you're on, at the reading line. You can also name a heading, or send a screenshot.

Each change shows in the prompter at once, with a note such as **Changed by Claude: "Pricing holds through March"** and an **Undo** button.

## What Claude can do

| Ask | What happens |
|---|---|
| "What cue am I on?", "Show me my cues" | Claude reads the page, every cue in order and the current one. |
| "Add a cue here about the price" | A new cue at the reading line, or at a heading you name. |
| "Shorten cue 3", "Reword the pricing cue" | Changes a cue's headline or points. |
| "Delete the churn cue" | Removes it. **Undo** in the prompter brings it back. |
| "Go to cue 5" | Scrolls the page and the prompter there. |
| "Turn off the section lines", "Bigger text", "Dark mode", "Pause" | Changes the view. |
| "Open the pricing page" | Opens that page, or switches to its tab, and the prompter follows. |

The full list, with each tool's name, is in [Claude tools](/guide/reference/claude-tools/).

<Aside type="note">
Live changes need the prompter to be open and connected to the page. If it isn't, Claude says so and asks you to click pagecue on the page.
</Aside>
