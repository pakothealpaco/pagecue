The [demo report](/demo/) is a made-up quarterly review that ships with six cues. Use it to see how presenting with pagecue feels before you write your own.

<Steps>

1. **Open the demo.** Go to [usepagecue.com/demo](/demo/) in Chrome.

2. **Open the prompter.** Click **pagecue** in the toolbar, or press <kbd>Alt</kbd> <kbd>Shift</kbd> <kbd>P</kbd>. A narrow window opens with the cues.

   Put it beside the report, or on a second screen.

3. **Scroll the report.** The prompter follows. The current cue is lit; the others are dimmed. The cue light at the top is green while the prompter follows the page.

4. **Drive it from the prompter.** Click a cue and the report scrolls there. Press <kbd>↓</kbd> or <kbd>Space</kbd> for the next cue, <kbd>↑</kbd> for the previous one. A presentation clicker works too.

5. **Change a word.** Double-click a cue, edit the headline or a point, and click away to save.

6. **See where each cue starts.** Press <kbd>L</kbd> to show section lines on the report. Drag a line's tag to move it. Press <kbd>L</kbd> again to hide the lines.

</Steps>

## Present it for real

In Zoom, Meet or Teams, share **the report's tab or window**, not your whole screen. Viewers see the report; the prompter stays with you. See [Share your screen privately](/guide/sharing/).

<Aside type="tip">
Section lines are drawn on the page, so viewers see them. Turn them off with <kbd>L</kbd> before you start.
</Aside>

## Now your own page

Ask Claude for cues on the page you're presenting: see [Add cues to a page](/guide/claude/add-cues/). When Claude builds the report in claude.ai or the Claude app, the cues come inside it: see [claude.ai and the Claude app](/guide/claude/apps/).

In Claude Code, there's a shortcut: open the page, click pagecue, and copy the command the prompter shows.
