Slides have presenter view. A website doesn't: when you share a report, a dashboard or a web app in a call, your notes are on paper, on a phone, or in your head. pagecue gives the page speaker notes of its own, in a small window that follows the page as you scroll and that you simply don't share.

## How it works

- **Your notes, called cues,** belong to parts of the page: one for the opening numbers, one for the chart, one for the ask.
- **The prompter** is a separate Chrome window that shows the cue for the part on screen. Scroll the page and the next cue lights up. Click a cue, or press <kbd>↓</kbd> or your clicker, and the page scrolls there.
- **You share the page's tab or window,** not your screen. Viewers see the website; the prompter stays with you.

## Set it up

<Steps>

1. **Install pagecue** for Chrome. It's free. See [Install](/guide/install/).

2. **Write the cues with Claude.** Give Claude the page and say what you want to get across: "Add speaker notes to this page. I'm presenting it to our partners on Thursday." Claude writes a short headline and up to three points for each part. See [Add cues to a page](/guide/claude/add-cues/).

3. **Open the page and click pagecue** in the toolbar, or press <kbd>Alt</kbd> <kbd>Shift</kbd> <kbd>P</kbd>. The prompter opens with your cues.

4. **Place the prompter** beside the page on one screen, close to your camera, or on a second screen.

5. **Share the page, not the screen.** In Google Meet, choose the Chrome tab. In Zoom or Teams, choose the Chrome window that holds the page. See [Share your screen privately](/guide/sharing/).

</Steps>

<Aside type="caution" title="Share a tab or a window">
If you share your entire screen, the prompter is on it like any other window. Share the page's tab or window instead.
</Aside>

## Which pages work

Any page Chrome opens: your own site, a client's site, a Claude artifact, a report on your computer, a web app with its own scrolling panels. Pages that show their content in a frame from another site ask for your permission once. See [Site access, frames and local files](/guide/sites/).
