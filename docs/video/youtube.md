> pagecue: speaker notes for any web page, written with Claude

**Description:**

```text
Presenting a report, dashboard or website on Zoom, Meet or Teams? pagecue gives you speaker notes for it, the way presenter view does for slides.

Ask Claude for speaker notes and it writes short cues for each part of the page. They appear in pagecue's prompter: a small window of its own that follows the page as you scroll. Share just the page's tab, and your notes stay with you.

• Claude writes the cues: in claude.ai, the Claude app or Claude Code
• The prompter follows the page; click a cue to jump there
• Double-click any cue to change its words
• Free and open source. No account; your cues stay on your computer

Get it: https://usepagecue.com
Guide: https://usepagecue.com/guide/
Source: https://github.com/pakothealpaco/pagecue
```
