# How pagecue talks

For the README, the product page, the store listing and the prompter itself.

## Who we're talking to

People who present what they made with Claude (reports, research, dashboards, sites) to partners, clients and their own team. Comfortable with Claude, not necessarily developers. They have a meeting soon and want to sound prepared.

## Personality

A calm colleague who has presented a hundred times: practical, specific, never selling hard.

| We are | We are not | Sounds like | Doesn't sound like |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Plain**: short words, one idea per sentence | dumbed down | "Share only the page. Your cues stay with you." | "Leverage a seamless presenter experience." |
| **Specific**: real numbers, real keys, real steps | technical for its own sake | "A headline of about eight words and up to three points." | "Glanceable, AI-optimised cue cards." |
| **Built with Claude**: Claude is the way cues get written and changed | "AI-powered", "Claude does it all" | "Tell Claude what you want to say; it writes the cues." | "Revolutionary AI that presents for you." |
| **Honest**: limits stated up front | apologetic | "Share the tab, not your whole screen." | "100% private, guaranteed." |

## Messaging pillars

1. **Speaker notes for any web page**: a presenter view for what isn't slides.
2. **Write your cues with Claude**: Claude plans the cues, writes them from your words and the page's facts, and changes them while you rehearse.
3. **Viewers see the page, not your cues**: the prompter is its own window; you share only the page.
4. **Yours**: local, no account, editable by hand, any language.

## Rules

- **Words to use:** cue (not note card or slide), prompter (the window), page (what you share), section lines, presenter view, speaker notes.
- **Words to avoid:** undetectable, invisible, secret, hidden from viewers (pagecue is a presenter aid, not a trick); AI-powered, seamless, revolutionary, empower, unlock, best.
- **No invented proof:** no invented users, quotes or numbers. Example numbers come from the fictional demo and are labelled as such.
- **Mechanics:** sentence case headings; contractions are fine; no exclamation marks; name keys with `<kbd>`.
