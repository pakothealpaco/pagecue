# Code of conduct

pagecue follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be kind and patient, assume good intent, welcome newcomers, and keep criticism about the work, not the person. Harassment, personal attacks and discriminatory language are not accepted in issues, pull requests or any other project space.

To report a problem, write to pakothealpaco@gmail.com. Reports are kept confidential. The maintainer may remove comments, close threads or block participants who break these rules.
